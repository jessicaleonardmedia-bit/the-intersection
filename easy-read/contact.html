Why would you get in touch?

  • You can tell us about a guest.

    A guest is someone who comes on the show to talk.

  • You can tell us an idea for the show.

  • You can ask about helping to make the show.

  • You can ask us anything else.

How do you get in touch?

  • You can send us an email.

  • Our email is hello@theintersectionshow.au

  • Or you can fill in the form on our Contact page.

  • The form asks for your name, your email and your message.

  • We will write back to you.
