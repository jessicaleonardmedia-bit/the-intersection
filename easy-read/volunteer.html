Volunteer with us

Go to the full Volunteer page

What is volunteering?

  • We want people to help make our TV show.

  • When you volunteer, you help without being paid.

  • We cannot pay anyone yet.

  • We hope to pay everyone soon.

  • We will credit you.

    This means we put your name on the show.

  • You learn how to make TV.

How much time does it take?

  • About 2 hours a week.

  • Some weeks more, some weeks less.

  • You choose how much you do.

Where do you work?

  • Most jobs are on a computer, from where you live.

  • We film the show in a TV studio in Melbourne.

  • You can come to the studio if you want.

Who can volunteer?

  • People who like what our show is about.

  • You can bring a skill you already have.

  • Or you can learn a new skill with us.

  • We want people from communities we don't often see on TV.

What jobs are there?

  • Finding money for the show.

  • Telling people about the show.

  • Helping on filming days.

  • Editing.

    Editing means putting the video together.

  • Finding out facts for the show.

  • Helping us stay organised.

  • You can pick one job or a few.

How do you put your hand up?

  • Fill in the form on our Volunteer page.

  • Tell us your name and how to contact you.

  • Tick the jobs you like.

  • You can send a video or voice message instead of writing.

  • You can ask for a phone or video chat instead.

  • Someone can help you fill in the form.

  • If you need help to apply, email hello@theintersectionshow.au

What happens next?

  • We read every form.

  • We cannot always write back to everyone.

  • If we can work with you, we will set up a chat.

  • You can ask us questions too.

  • Then we work out the right job for you together.
