About the show

Go to the full About page

What is The Intersection?

  • This is the Easy Read version of our About page.

  • The Intersection is a TV show. People sit at a desk and talk.

  • Each show is about something from everyday life.

  • We think about how the different parts of a person's life fit together.

    This is called being intersectional.

  • We invite guests whose stories are not often on TV.

  • We use jokes, not hard words, to talk about big topics.

Who makes the show?

  • Three women from Melbourne make the show.

  • Jess and Shaz are the hosts.

    Hosts lead the talk on the show.

  • Jess and Shaz grew up in very different ways.

  • They are good friends.

    They like to talk about big ideas.

Where can you watch it?

  • The show is not on TV yet.

    We are still making it.

  • When it is finished, it will be on C31 in Melbourne and Geelong.

    It will also be on C44 in Adelaide.

  • You can also watch it on your phone or tablet, on the CTV+ app.

  • You can follow us on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.

What do we hope for?

  • We want everyone to be kind to each other.

    This includes people who are different from us.

  • We make TV for everyday people.

    We want it to help people learn and be kind.

  • You can read more on our full About page.
